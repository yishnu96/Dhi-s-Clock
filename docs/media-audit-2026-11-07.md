## Editorial decisions

Clock reveal photographs and both videos from `dia_info` are placed on the clock page based on their content. The previous “Out in the world” section and its old launch collection are removed from the page. The source folders are preserved.

Campaign illustrations are identified as campaign material, not customer testimonials. Clippings are presented as supplied records, not scientific validation. Award artwork is described as archive material without inventing an award title or status. No medical-effectiveness claims or interview speech transcripts have been added.

Republic World’s original interview was found at https://www.republicworld.com/initiatives/tete-a-tete-with-saudamini-mishra-aka-dhi-inventor-of-dhi-s-clock-people-s-digital-mentor-and-prot and linked beside its clipping. Other clippings link to the full supplied image.

## Delivery

Full WebP images are capped at 1400 pixels; thumbnails at 640, preserving aspect ratios without upscaling. Original H.264/AAC MP4s are 504 × 696 pixels, approximately 25 and 23 seconds. Videos use manual playback, preload none and generated posters. No transcript is claimed. Placement order and concise display titles are in `tools/media-placement.mjs`.
