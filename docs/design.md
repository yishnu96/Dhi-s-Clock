# Dhi’s Clock redesign

Approved direction: author-and-inventor website that builds confidence in Saudamini Mishra and makes contacting her easy. Approved in the chat on 2026-11-06.

Audience: curious visitors and people considering contacting Dhi, including those seeking her background and achievements. Primary action: Contact Dhi. Book sales and service booking are future scope.

Preserve the source website’s information, gallery, logo story, recognition, two blog links, email and phone. Rewrite for readability while attributing philosophical and preventive claims to the creator’s intentions, not independently proven outcomes. No invented services, awards, reviews, dates or credentials.

Visual direction: monochrome editorial, generous negative space, expressive serif headings, small restrained sans-serif navigation, original images only. Light mode by default unless the device prefers dark; remembered toggle. An original gallery image carries the hero. Responsive compositions for 320px upward. Smooth native scrolling, short entrance and interaction animations, reduced-motion support. No heavy animation library, autoplay video or canvas.

Approaches: biography-first (too little explanation of the work), product-first (underplays the person), trust-first author/inventor (chosen by user). The latter connects the original material in a visitor-friendly sequence.

Architecture: static HTML, local CSS, vanilla JS. No runtime dependencies. Local compressed images and self-hosted fonts. Accessible native navigation, details and image dialog; real email and phone links. Existing blog destinations retained.

SEO: semantic content, title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Person/CreativeWork structured data, robots and sitemap. Domain metadata assumes the rebuilt site eventually replaces dhisclock.com; README explains adjustment before other-domain publication.

Research: https://www.chani.com/app explains features concretely and shows the product; https://www.thepattern.com/ leads with personal understanding and then describes use cases. Borrow clarity and sequence only. No reference-site assets or claims.

Scope: local, ready-to-host deliverable. Publication is separate.
